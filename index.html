Who we are

Technology Driven by Purpose. Innovation Driven by Vision.

MJAY is an IT and technology solutions company based in Bangalore, one of India’s leading innovation hubs. We combine modern software engineering, AI and automation to turn complex business challenges into practical solutions, and we believe intelligent technology should be affordable for businesses of every size.

Read More About Us

Our solutions are built on clean engineering, practical AI and a clear understanding of your business. Whether it is a web platform, a mobile app or an intelligent automation, every project is shaped around your goals, delivered on time and made to grow with you.

From our base in Bangalore, we work closely with startups, growing companies and established enterprises to modernise their systems, automate repetitive work and unlock the value hidden in their data. We start by listening, then design, build and support solutions that are secure, scalable and simple to use.

We believe good technology should never be out of reach. That is why we keep our process transparent, our pricing fair and our focus on one thing: helping your business move faster, work smarter and grow with confidence.
